01 — Introduction

A brand that should be seen online, too.

The Grid sells visibility for a living. Your own social presence should set the standard — showcasing your screens, your points of presence and your results, while generating enquiries from the brands you want to work with.

This proposal sets out how Lubelo Tech Solutions will plan, produce and manage The Grid's social media — on-brand to your CI guide, consistent with your new website, and built to grow both authority and advertiser leads.

  • Credibility & authority — a marketing agency is judged by its own marketing
  • Showcase the network — billboards in-situ, airport trays, the journey, real campaigns
  • B2B lead generation — reach marketers and brand managers, especially on LinkedIn
  • Proof & social proof — client wins, data and results, told visually
  • Consistency — one voice across website and social, drawn from your CI guide
