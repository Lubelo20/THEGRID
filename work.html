The Way We Work

Closed-loop.
End-to-end.
Strategically aligned.

The Grid is an ecosystem-style advertising agency. We present a closed-loop, journey-style value proposition — end-to-end points of presence along a strategically defined consumer journey, maximising impact and delivering measurable ROI. From the morning commute to the airport, the mall, and home again, your brand stays top of mind at every waypoint.

Mission

To deliver impactful, data-informed and highly targeted out-of-home media solutions — with precision and professionalism.

Vision

To become South Africa's leading Black-owned OOH media company — driving innovation, access and inclusivity.

Strategic Journey Mapping

We place your brand at every waypoint of your audience's day — home, commute, airport, mall.

Data-Driven Intelligence

Location data, footfall and impression modelling — attribution, not estimates.

Our Story

Built to turn spaces into journeys.

The Grid Technologies is a Johannesburg-born, 100% Black-owned out-of-home media company. We were built on one conviction: a billboard isn't a destination — it's a single moment in someone's day.

So we stopped selling isolated spaces and started building journeys. Across digital screens, airport touchpoints and large-format static, we map how your audience actually moves and meet them at every meaningful waypoint — guided by data, sharpened by creative. The result is an ecosystem, not an ad: measurable presence that compounds from the morning commute to the boarding gate to the mall and home again.

We don't just find your brand a space — we craft strategic, data-driven brand journeys that live in the real world.
