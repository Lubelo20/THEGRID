The Way We Work

Closed-loop.
End-to-end.
Strategically aligned.

The Grid is an ecosystem-style advertising agency. We present a closed-loop, journey-style value proposition — end-to-end points of presence along a strategically defined consumer journey, maximising impact and delivering measurable ROI. From the morning commute to the gym, the mall, the airport, and home again, your brand stays top of mind at every waypoint.

Mission

To deliver impactful, data-informed and highly targeted out-of-home media solutions — with precision and professionalism.

Vision

To become South Africa's leading Black-owned OOH media company — driving innovation, access and inclusivity.

Strategic Journey Mapping

We place your brand at every waypoint of your audience's day — home, commute, gym, mall, airport.

Data-Driven Intelligence

Location data, footfall and impression modelling — attribution, not estimates.

Premium Presence Network

High-LSM commercial zones and South Africa's three busiest international airports.

The Closed Loop

One journey,
five waypoints.

Home → Gym → Mall → Airport → Home. We sequence your brand across the day so each touchpoint reinforces the last — building recall and driving measurable conversion.

01 · Home
01 — Home

It starts before they leave.

Top-of-mind recall on the morning commute. The Witkoppen Road static billboard reaches thousands of AB-income drivers between Fourways and Sandton before the day has even begun.

02 · Gym
02 — Health & Wellness

Reinforced mid-workout.

A unique in-gym advertising experience across the Planet Fitness network — an active, high-LSM audience with 45–90 minute dwell times, enhancing the member experience while your brand stays in view.

03 · Mall
03 — Mall

Closed at the point of intent.

DOOH at Dainfern Square — plus the iconic 10m × 70m static on Winnie Mandela Drive — meets high-LSM shoppers in-venue, in the moments that drive the purchase.

04 · Airport
04 — Airport

Captured at peak attention.

Security-tray branding reaches 21M+ passengers a year — 30 to 60 seconds of undivided, hands-on attention, with zero competition for the eye.

05 — Home again. Repeated, sequenced touchpoints compound into recall, conversion and measurable ROI — and the loop begins anew.
