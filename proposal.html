03 — What We've Built

The mockup, page by page.

A fast, dependency-free, fully responsive site spanning eight pages with rich interactivity.

Home

  • Cinematic particle intro splash that forms "The Grid", then reveals the site (shown once per visit, skippable, reduced-motion safe)
  • Video hero with positioning statement and primary CTAs
  • Animated key-stat counters, "explore the ecosystem" pillars, closed-loop journey video, scrolling client wall, and conversion band

Way of Work

  • Mission & vision, strategic pillars, "our story", a type-on animated positioning quote over a brand image, and the Home → Airport → Mall → Home journey told visually

Services & Data

  • Five flip cards — Digital Billboards (DOOH), Airport Security Trays, Static Large-Format, Mobile Billboards and Brand Activations — that flip on hover and tap
  • Dedicated Data & Analytics page: why data matters, how it's captured → measured → attributed → optimised, and what we measure

Points of Presence (POPs)

  • Interactive journey stepper, location cards, and an animated map of South Africa's provinces with geographically-placed, colour-coded pins that reveal each location on tap

Clients

  • Scrolling client wall and a Success Stories section with animated bar & trend charts set over a real campaign photo (figures illustrative until verified data is supplied)

Team

  • Hover/tap capability cards plus a "why brands trust us" credibility section and testimonial

Contact

  • Direct phone, email, WhatsApp, campaign brief form and an embedded location map; social links consolidated in the footer
04 — Build Quality

Engineered properly.

Responsive & mobile

  • Fluid layouts from large desktop to phone
  • Collapsing hamburger navigation
  • Tap-friendly flip cards & map pins
  • Mobile-specific refinements

Accessibility

  • Keyboard navigation & focus states
  • Skip links, ARIA labels, live regions
  • Honours "reduced motion" preferences

Performance

  • Lightweight, no heavy frameworks
  • Lazy-loaded media, optimised assets
  • Fast first paint

SEO foundations

  • Per-page titles & meta descriptions
  • Open Graph / social share cards
  • Sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, favicon

Tech approach: hand-built HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript — no bloated page-builders. The result is fast, secure, easy to maintain and fully owned by The Grid.
