How It Works

From presence
to proof.

Data runs through every stage of the closed loop — not bolted on at the end, but built into how we plan, place and refine your campaign.

1 · Capture

Every placement is geo- and demographically mapped to your target audience before a single rand is spent.

2 · Measure

Footfall, impressions and dwell time are tracked across the network in real conditions — not modelled guesses.

3 · Attribute

We connect exposure to outcomes — store visits, sign-ups, conversions — with transparent attribution modelling.

4 · Optimise

Insights feed back into the loop: we refine placement and creative to compound performance across the campaign.
