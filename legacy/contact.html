Common Questions

Everything you need
to know before booking.

What is the minimum campaign duration?
We typically recommend a minimum of 4 weeks per placement to allow for meaningful audience reach and frequency. However, we can accommodate shorter bursts for specific activations, particularly at airport locations where events or peaks may require shorter windows.
How does the closed-loop strategy work?
We map your target audience's daily consumer journey — from home to commute, airport, mall, and back — then place your brand at strategic waypoints along that route. The result is a campaign that intercepts the same audience multiple times per day, across different mindsets and environments.
What data do you provide post-campaign?
We provide comprehensive post-campaign reports including impression counts, audience demographics, footfall data (where applicable), screen uptime logs, and attribution analysis. For airport placements, we can provide passenger volume data correlated with your campaign period.
Do you handle the creative production?
Our Creative Director can assist with artwork briefing, format specification, and creative guidance. For full production, we work with trusted creative partners or can brief your existing agency with our format requirements to ensure your creative performs optimally across all placements.
Which industries do you work with most?
Our network is particularly well-suited to financial services, FMCG, automotive, travel & hospitality, retail, and luxury brands — all sectors where reaching high-LSM audiences at multiple touchpoints drives meaningful ROI. That said, our strategy works for any brand targeting premium South African consumers.
How quickly can a campaign go live?
Once artwork is approved and bookings are confirmed, DOOH campaigns can typically go live within 5–7 business days. Airport tray campaigns require slightly longer lead times (2–3 weeks) to coordinate with airport authority requirements. We'll give you a precise timeline in your campaign proposal.
