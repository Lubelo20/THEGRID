03 — Hosting, domain & go-live

Getting it online.

The site will be deployed to The Grid's own company hosting. To publish it, we need access to that hosting and to the domain's DNS. It's a static, dependency-free site, so it runs on any standard web host — no special server software required.

Go-live sequence

Once Wave 1 content is in and the site is signed off at MVP, launch is a short, controlled cutover:

#Step
1Final content, images & integrations loaded; full QA and cross-device / cross-browser test pass.
2Contact form, Analytics tag and WhatsApp button wired and tested end-to-end.
3Deploy to The Grid's company hosting (via cPanel / FTP); enable & verify SSL.
4Point thegridtech.co.za DNS at the live site (website records only — email untouched).
5Switch the site from "hidden" to publicly indexable (the mockup is deliberately hidden from Google today).
6Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console; confirm Analytics is receiving data.
7Final live smoke-test on desktop & mobile — go-live confirmed.

Note on the current preview

The mockup you're reviewing is intentionally set to "no-index" so search engines don't list it before launch. Flipping it to public is a one-line change we make as the final go-live step (step 5 above).
