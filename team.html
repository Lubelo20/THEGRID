Our Story

Built to turn spaces into journeys.

The Grid Technologies is a Johannesburg-born, 100% Black-owned out-of-home media company. We were built on one conviction: a billboard isn't a destination — it's a single moment in someone's day.

So we stopped selling isolated spaces and started building journeys. Across digital screens, airport touchpoints and large-format static, we map how your audience actually moves and meet them at every meaningful waypoint — guided by data, sharpened by creative. The result is an ecosystem, not an ad: measurable presence that compounds from the morning commute to the gym, the mall, the boarding gate and home again.
