The Ecosystem Advantage

Closed-loop.
End-to-end.
Always on.

The Grid is not a billboard company. We are an ecosystem-style advertising and marketing agency presenting clients with a closed-loop, journey-style value proposition — end-to-end points of presence based on waypoints along a strategically defined consumer journey.

Our diverse offering delivers value across multiple mediums of engagement, keeping your brand top-of-mind at every critical touchpoint — from the morning commute billboard, through airport security, to the in-mall DOOH screen, and home again.

Strategic Journey Mapping
We identify where your audience lives, travels, shops and works, then place your brand at every waypoint along that journey.
Data-Driven Intelligence
Audience measurement, footfall data, and impression modelling deliver real-world evidence of campaign performance — attribution, not estimates.
Premium Presence Network
Our curated network spans Johannesburg's highest-LSM commercial zones and South Africa's three busiest international airports.
